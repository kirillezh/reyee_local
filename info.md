# Ruijie Reyee (Local)

Local Home Assistant integration for Ruijie Reyee EG-series gateways,
NBS managed switches and ES-series PoE switches. Monitor connected devices per
VLAN, track clients, watch WAN/public IP, and control WAN failover, port
forwarding and bandwidth. Add switches with + Add switch on the router entry (they group under Switches) for per-port status,
VLANs, port on/off, PoE on/off, PoE power-cycle and PoE power monitoring. All over
your LAN, no cloud. Tested on EG105G-V3, NBS3100-24GT4SFP-V2, RG-ES220GS-P,
RG-ES205GC-P, RG-ES210GS-P, RG-ES208GC and RG-ES206GS-P (ReyeeOS 2.x).
